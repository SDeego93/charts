Important tax information

Tax year: 2026. This tool assumes US individual ownership, taxable positive rental profit, one state for all listed properties, and the entered expenses are deductible. It does not calculate actual state brackets, state deductions, city/county income taxes, QBI deductions, passive-activity or at-risk rules, depreciation recapture, capital gains, alternative minimum tax, net investment income tax, self-employment tax, credits, nonresident taxation, business/entity taxes, personal-use allocations, or property tax. Ordinary rental income is commonly reported on Schedule E, but substantial guest services can change its tax treatment. A dwelling used as a home and rented for fewer than 15 days may qualify for a special federal exclusion; that exemption is not automatically calculated here. Zero lodging tax entered or displayed does not prove exemption. Get professional advice before filing or remitting.

Sources: IRS 2026 federal brackets · Tax Foundation 2026 state income-tax rates · IRS rental-property rules · Airbnb tax collection locations. State reference rates use Tax Foundation's 2026 schedule, updated for later 2026 legislation in Arkansas, Georgia, South Carolina, Utah and West Virginia. Verify changes before publication or reuse in future tax years.
